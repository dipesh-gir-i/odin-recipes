Pan-Seared Chicken Tenderloin

Description

This pan-seared chicken tenderloin recipe teaches you how to cook chicken tenderloins with a simple combination of seasonings, olive oil, and lemon juice, which makes the chicken delicious!

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken tenderloins
  • 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt, or to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper, or to taste
  • 2 teaspoons paprika, or more to taste
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder, or more to taste
  • 2 teaspoons onion powder, or more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon Cajun seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • ½ lemon, juiced

Steps

  1. Place chicken in a large bowl. Add 3 tablespoons oil, salt, and pepper; turn to coat. Add paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, Cajun seasoning, and chili powder. Turn to coat.
  2. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Add chicken, cover, and cook, about 6 minutes. Pour lemon juice over chicken. Flip and cook until a dark, crispy crust forms, about 6 minutes more.
